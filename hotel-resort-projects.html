Hotel nets: indoor or outdoor

How to install your outdoor or indoor hotel net?

Elegant, sturdy outdoor nets for your hotel. All our nets are UV-treated for greater resistance and to ensure their longevity in extreme environments (sea spray, high sun exposure, humidity).

Installation is simple and professional: the edges are welded and sewn with eyelets every 15cm, allowing for easy tensioning with maritime-grade ropes.

Whether you choose the 30x30mm mesh for its transparency or the 40x40mm mesh for its supreme barefoot comfort, Damannets provides all the technical support needed for a perfect setup.

The materials of our nets for luxury hotels

Our hotel nets are knotless for maximum comfort. They are soft to the touch and don't mark the skin. The edges of our hotel nets are welded and sewn with eyelets or sheaths for a perfect finish and easy installation.
