Guardrail, safety and anti-fall nets

Uncompromising Safety meets Aesthetic Design

For indoor or outdoor use, our custom-made safety nets are the ideal choice for safety railings, designer staircases, and effective fall protection. They provide a modern, minimalist look while ensuring maximum security.

Damannets guardrail nets combine attractive design with extreme resistance. They are easy to install, UV-resistant, and meet the strict EN1263-1 safety standard for professional fall protection.

Simple Installation in 3 Steps

1

Install Fixings

Anchor eye bolts or hooks into your frame (wood, concrete, or metal).

2

Position the Net

Attach the corners of the net to your fixings using carabiners or rope.

3

Tension & Lace

Lace the tensioning rope through the net edging and fixings for a tight fit.
